OP Game Hub

Privacy

The short version: you never need an account to play. If you sign in, we keep a display name and the month you joined. That's it.

Playing without signing in

Every game works without an account. Your progress, coins and unlocks are saved in your own browser on your own device. We don't see them.

If you sign in with Google

We keep:

  • An account number Google gives us for you, so we know it's you next time. It isn't your email or your name.
  • The display name you pick, like @icebot566.
  • The date you joined. Your profile shows only the month.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for 30 days on that browser.

We don't keep your email, your real name, your photo, your age or where you live. Google shows us your email during sign-in, and we throw it away straight away.

Who can see what

Anyone can see your display name and the month you joined on your profile page. Nobody can message you: there is no chat on OP Game Hub. Profile pages are hidden from search engines.

Deleting your account

Open your profile and press Delete my account. Your display name and account are removed straight away. You can also remove OP Game Hub from your Google account in your Google account settings.

For parents

Signing in is optional, and players under 18 should ask a parent or guardian first. We don't show ads, sell data, or let players talk to each other. Our sign-in and database run on Cloudflare, and sign-in uses Google.
