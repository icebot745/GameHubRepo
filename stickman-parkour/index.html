Stickman Parkour: a free neon platformer

Run, jump and flip across glowing rooftops as a neon stickman. Clear 50 levels, chase your best distance in Endless Mode, and spend the coins you earn on skins, gear, music and overpowered power-ups. It is free, it plays right in your browser, and it works on phones, tablets and computers.

How to play

  • Run: arrow keys or A / D. On a phone, use the arrow buttons.
  • Jump: Space, W or the up arrow. Hold for a higher jump; let go of forward mid-air to drop straight down.
  • Power-ups: keys 1 to 7, or tap the buttons at the top of the screen.
  • Goal: reach the green finish flag. Orange flags are checkpoints. Pink spikes and falling send you back.

What's inside

  • 50 levels plus Endless Mode (3 coins every 10 m)
  • 7 block types: solid, moving, bounce pads, crumble, ice, conveyors and blink blocks
  • 7 power-ups: Jump Boost, Speed, Flight, Double Jump, Shield, Slow-Mo and Double Coins
  • 3 difficulties and 20 rebirths for permanent speed, jump and coin boosts
  • Shop: 14 skins, 32 accessories for head, body, hands and feet, 8 music tracks and 4 sound packs

Questions

Is Stickman Parkour free?

Yes. Stickman Parkour is 100% free to play in your web browser. There is nothing to buy with real money. Coins are earned only by playing.

Do I need to download anything or sign up?

No. The game runs right in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There is no download, no app store and no account.

Does Stickman Parkour save my progress?

Yes. Your coins, unlocked levels, skins and rebirths save automatically in your browser on that device. Clearing your browser data, or switching to another device or browser, starts a fresh save.

Is Stickman Parkour safe for kids?

Yes. There is no chat, no sign-up and no personal information collected. It is a single-player game with cartoon stickman characters and no violence.

How do you play Stickman Parkour on a phone?

On a phone or tablet, on-screen buttons appear: left and right arrows to run, a big jump button, and power-up buttons at the top of the screen.

What are rebirths in Stickman Parkour?

A rebirth costs 1,000 coins and resets your coins, power-ups and level progress, but permanently gives your stickman 7.5% more speed, 7.5% higher jumps and 7.5% more coins from levels and Endless Mode. You can rebirth up to 20 times.
